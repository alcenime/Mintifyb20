01

What is B20?

B20 is the token standard that $MINTIFY is issued under. It defines how the token behaves — how it's minted, held, and transferred on Base.

02

Why B20?

It keeps things simple. No unnecessary complexity, no excess mechanics — just a clean standard built for an experimental project that wants to move fast.

03

How it relates to Base

Base is the network. B20 runs on top of it, taking advantage of Base's speed and low cost to make minting accessible and affordable.

04

How it relates to Mintify

$MINTIFY is the token at the center of the Mintify project. B20 is the standard it's built on — the two are inseparable.

ABOUT MINTIFY

We mint things.

Mintify is an experimental project built on Base. We treat minting as a way of thinking — a way of taking an idea seriously enough to make it permanent.

Everything starts as an experiment. Some become tokens, some become stories, some don't survive past the sketch. What they share is a single destination: onchain.

$MINTIFY, our token, runs on the B20 standard — a simple foundation that keeps the project honest and easy to build on. Base gives us the speed and cost to make that experimentation actually possible.

Mintify isn't a finished product. It's an ongoing practice of building in public, one mint at a time.
